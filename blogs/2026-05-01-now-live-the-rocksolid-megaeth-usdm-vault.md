Today, RockSolid launched the RockSolid MegaETH USDm Vault in partnership with MegaETH. This vault enables users to deposit USDC on mainnet and seamlessly generate yields on MegaETH with USDm while the vault deploys into MegaETH DeFi. This marks the third RockSolid DeFi vault and the first RockSolid branded stablecoin vault.
